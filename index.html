Five primary screens

Why every scan ends in a review

A model can recognize a carton of eggs. It can't reliably know how many eggs are inside a closed carton, or read an expiry date from a label it can't see. So the scanner suggests and the person confirms. Nothing is saved until they do.

Scanning is optional. Quick manual entry and barcode scanning are always one tap away.

Add groceries from a photo

  1. Tap Scan Groceries and photograph your groceries on the counter.
  2. The image is compressed and sent securely to the backend.
  3. AI returns structured suggestions: product name, possible quantity, category and confidence.
  4. A review screen lets the user correct names, quantities, storage locations and dates.
  5. Once confirmed, PantryPal updates inventory and refreshes recipe recommendations.
